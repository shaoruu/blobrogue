# blobrogue — STUDIO CREATIVE COHERENCE GATE
**Creative Director recommendation to the main project runner.** Final calls/routing remain with blobrogue.

## The hierarchy (what the game is allowed to be about)
1. **Emotional core:** a soft, living amber hero leaves a warm home and descends into a cold world that is losing material, warmth, and memory.
2. **Combat core:** expressive weapons + blessings + four statuses + combo + one universal Resonance meter.
3. **World spine:** six authored regions that escalate one corruption story using the same movement, hazard, destructible, and telegraph foundations.
4. **Progression:** bosses open places and horizontal options; Amber visibly grows home; gear specializes; mastery proves skill.
5. **Recognition:** profiles/records show what the player has done. They never become another power economy.

Anything that cannot serve one of those five jobs is cut or deferred.

---
## Strongest structural call: FIVE bosses, SIX regions
Active canon currently defines four biomes, not six. Do not pretend Null exists until it has a role, material, mobs, hazard, gate, and final fight.

Use **five mandatory bosses as gates between six regions**. Do not add a sixth mandatory boss just to make the numbers match. Region five is a deliberate threshold/endurance biome with an optional lieutenant; Jet becomes the Null finale.

### 1. AMBERWILD (rename Verdant Hollow)
**Role:** living boundary around Amber Camp; the last place where the world still grows correctly. Teaches flocking, elastic motion, destructibles, and readable recovery.

Why rename: “Verdant Hollow” collides with **The Hollow**, our flagship corruption vocabulary. Reserve Hollow for drained amber.

**Material:** wet roots, leaf mats, damp wood, soft amber sap. Green is the world lane; hero amber stays warmer/brighter.

**Mobs:** Slime (FLOCK→SURGE), Bat as a light harasser; Rootkite only later as an elite if Pack Slime needs more silhouette variety.

**Signature interaction:** **Snaproot** — coils, points, commits, then springs entities/projectiles; after firing it lies slack and can be destroyed. Same universal hazard contract, no new meter.

**Boss:** **The Slime King, First of the Fallen** — tragic flock-summoner, the first blob that accumulated too much living amber and could no longer return home.

**Unlock:** Sunless Caves + secondary weapon slot + burn/chill/shock blessings. Camp gains an overgrown trophy/display and the first strong Amber Heart beat.

### 2. SUNLESS CAVES
**Role:** warmth is absent; sound and momentum replace sight. Teaches listening, last-known-position commitment, wall impacts, and baiting.

**Material:** resonant shale, exposed bone, dust, dead lantern housings. Blue/cold lane. Do not use amber/orange as the default danger color; reserve scarce amber for lost-home traces and use bone-white/red pressure tells.

**Mobs:** Orbit-Dive Bat; **Knellbat** as an elite leader, not a separate common family; **Rattleback** as the burrowing listener.

**Signature interaction:** **Echo Shale** — stepping/shooting it emits an authoritative noise event and a directional fracture lane. Players can deliberately mislead listeners. Breaks after one strong impact.

**Boss:** **Marrow** — sound-commit gatekeeper. Rename move Gore-Charge → **Soundbreak**. Derive hearing from authoritative velocity/noise events, never raw input.

**Unlock:** The Deep + second addon/trinket + The Hollow family/Fracture. Camp gains a listening post / resonant shale instrument, not another currency station.

### 3. THE DEEP
**Role:** corruption gains physical structure. Wrong geometry is no longer magic; it is black resin seams, fractured architecture, and surfaces that no longer agree on “down.”

**Material:** jet resin, cold mineral, broken load-bearing forms, dead amber. Purple becomes this region’s specific hue lane, not the whole game.

**Mobs:** **Crookleg** (wall-pounce) and later **Seamwalker** (visible path controller). Never introduce both in the same first room. Rattleback can recur sparingly as shared ecology.

**Signature interaction:** **Load Seam** — a visible resin fracture banks impact from bullets/melee, then snaps directionally, moving debris or opening/closing a lane. It reuses Fracture’s material language without applying the combat status to the world.

**Boss:** **The Weaver** — keeper of fracture paths / pattern-reading duelist. All displacement travels along visible mineral/resin seams. Slow builds get secondary punish windows through lattice destruction/stagger.

**Unlock:** Emberreach + precision/mobility sidegrades + first family mastery challenges + broader Rare blessing options. No second boss in The Deep.

### 4. EMBERREACH
**Role:** **false warmth.** Heat here comes from pressure, friction, and trapped grief, not life. This preserves warm Amber Camp vs. cold corruption: Emberreach glows, but it does not comfort.

**Material:** clinker, slag, vitrified floor, pressure vents, cold cyan-white stress cores beneath red/orange surfaces.

**Mobs:** **Bellows** = ANCHOR, generates a directional thermal lane and then deflates/repositions. **Cinderjack** = HUNT, commits via jet burst and exposes a cooling core. Keep both; their room decisions are distinct.

**Signature interaction:** **Pressure Vent** — authored vent points, commits to a lane, then cools into temporary cover/route. Destructible caps can redirect the next burst. Same universal hazard phases.

**Boss:** rename **The Hollow Choir → The Ashen Choir**. “Hollow” now belongs to the weapon family; Ashen preserves grief and Emberreach material. It is a grieving raid-artillery boss, not another generic summoner. Replace swarm adds with a few song-linked **voice fragments**. Replace target rings/full radial bursts with fulgurite cracks, uneven sung pressure wedges, and vitrifying heat seams.

**Unlock:** Gilded Scar + Choir gear + repeatable contracts/mastery cosmetics + a Camp **Listening Hall/Memorial**. It does not unlock “Emberreach events” from inside Emberreach.

### 5. GILDED SCAR (threshold biome; NO mandatory boss)
**Role:** failed amber civilization. The player sees home’s material made rigid, archival, and imprisoning. This is the warning between hostile heat and total subtraction.

**Material:** dead honey, tarnished brass, cold white mineral, fossil plates. Never use Camp’s living pulse/glow.

**Mobs:** anchored fossil constructs using existing ANCHOR/HUNT/FLOCK modules. Do not introduce a new movement system. Reuse Goldwork deployables only after their player-side rules exist.

**Signature interaction:** **Dead Prism** — authored fossil lens can be rotated once or shattered; redirects one pressure lane/projectile family, then remains as cover or debris. Same hazard contract.

**Lieutenant (optional):** demote Gilded Warden to **The Amber Bailiff**, a post-Choir Goldwork mastery encounter. Not a sixth mandatory boss. Replace glyph/rune/prison rings with interlocking fossil shelves, resin seams, and embedded relic silhouettes.

**Unlock hook:** clearing the Scar and breaking its final seal opens Null. Defeating the optional Bailiff unlocks Goldwork family + Camp construction cosmetics/functions. Goldwork no longer depends on a mandatory sixth boss.

### 6. NULL
**Role:** subtraction, not “another dark biome.” Texture, warmth, ambience, and reliable geometry have been consumed. The player recognizes earlier systems by what is missing.

**Material:** soot, cold blue-black, dead-resin brown, tiny dying-amber seams. Sparse authored forms; never procedural emptiness or low-contrast unreadability.

**Mobs:** final sparse remixes of the six universal movement verbs. Their bodies may lose material detail, but committed silhouettes/telegraphs remain fully readable. No new status, currency, or Null meter.

**Signature interaction:** **Blank Shear** — a clearly authored seam removes a piece of cover/hazard affordance after a directional tell, then leaves a stable empty lane. It subtracts an existing rule; it does not add a subsystem.

**Final boss:** **Jet** — black fossil resin, the cold reflection of living amber. Curated, counter-readable family rotation; never copies the whole player build. Its Resonance expression uses The Light Goes Out’s original dead-amber material language, not violet curse art.

**Final unlock:** family Fusions + adaptive mastery challenges + visible restoration/evolution of the Amber Heart. Endgame opens through mastery, not a vertical level ladder.

**Mandatory boss chain:**
`Slime King → Marrow → Weaver → Ashen Choir → Jet`

## Mob-family gate
Keep the universal verbs: HUNT / ORBIT / BURROW / ANCHOR / FLOCK / FLEE-BAIT.

**Ship order stays:** Bat → Slime → Rattleback → Crookleg. This cheaply proves four verbs with shared tech.

**Wave 2:** Knellbat first; Seamwalker second; Bellows and Cinderjack when pressure hazards exist. Rootkite merges into a Verdant elite Pack-Slime expression unless playtests prove a separate family is needed.

Rules:
- One primary verb + at most one escalation modifier.
- Max two complex movement archetypes per room.
- Silhouette predicts verb at rest.
- Material evidence visualizes danger; internal radii never justify circle/ring filler.
- Knellbat is a Bat-family leader. Rattleback and Marrow sharing sound ecology is intentional. Crookleg and Seamwalker never debut together.

---
## Hazards + destructible world: ONE foundation
Do not build six hazard engines. All world interactions use:

`material source → directional tell → committed footprint → active pressure → recovery/cooling → optional destruction/weaponization`

Biome expressions are Snaproot / Echo Shale / Load Seam / Pressure Vent / Dead Prism / Blank Shear.

**Cut/defer:** gas fog, secret walls, advanced brazier fire, and broad freeform environmental systems until one signature interaction ships well end-to-end. Gas risks becoming a fifth status. Generic crates/pots/barrels may remain as baseline utility, but each biome needs one authored material prop family; do not scatter identical dungeon furniture through all six regions.

Explosive barrels remain because they already obey the contract and create chain interaction. Brazier remains dressing until it has a specific authored job.

## Progression + account loop (one job per incentive)
### Integrated loop
1. Wake in **Amber Camp**. Amber Heart and inhabitants physically show progression.
2. At the **Armorer**, choose two weapons, two addons, and up to three bounded Heartwork choices.
3. At the **Amber Heart**, arm a family Resonance expression. It is not another meter.
4. Enter Descent/Wilds Gate.
5. During expedition: Lv1–3 blessings, temporary Coins, Dealer decisions, sidegrade discoveries, 4–6× expressive build through synergy.
6. Boss: next place + one horizontal option + one authored relic choice + modest Amber + canonical first-kill flag.
7. Return: temporary build clears, Amber/discoveries bank, Camp visibly changes.
8. Endgame: Archive contracts/mastery → behavior variants, cosmetics, trophies, at most one Fusion. No mastery XP.

### Camp names/merges
- **Amber Camp** is canonical. Remove “Blob Camp.”
- **Armorer** contains Weapon Rack + Addon Bench + bounded Foundation loadout.
- **Practice Yard** replaces duplicate Training/Practice Range names.
- Resonance selection is physically part of the **Amber Heart**, not a separate shrine.
- **Archive** owns contracts, mastery, personal records, and later Jet-gated Fusion research. Fusion Forge is an Archive expansion, not a sixth building.
- **Dealer** previews expedition stock only; Coins cannot be spent in Camp.
- **Healer** is a free reset/testing service in Camp, not another economy.

### Dealer hook
Do not leave “Dealer” as a vending machine every third floor. Make **Patch**, a warm amber salvage-hauler who follows routes the player has already cleared. Their fold-out cabinet is built from recovered doors/prop pieces. They appear at depths 3/6/9 because those are secured relay niches, not because a shop room materializes. This needs no new currency or reputation system.

### Foundation reshape
Keep the 20–30% total ceiling but remove generic rank trees.
- Rename equipped permanent choices **Heartwork** (work done to/with the Amber Heart, not ritual “rites”).
- One choice per category; no ranks. Cut Tempered Shot II.
- Prefer mechanical/bounded choices over mandatory damage: +1 heart OR movement/dash recovery OR small starting-coin utility; raw damage remains one modest option, never the obvious default.
- Competitive contracts normalize/disable Heartwork.

### Pets
**Cut combat pets.** They duplicate summons/turrets/co-op silhouettes and create a slot, collectible track, AI burden, and likely pet currency.

If desired later, use **Camp Companions** only: cosmetic inhabitants earned from a boss/mastery flag. No stats, rarity, level, slot, currency, or combat behavior.

### Account unlocks
Account unlocks are authored content flags: boss kills, regions, families, discoveries, Camp nodes, mastery proof. No sprawling achievement economy. `totalCoins` is labeled **Coins earned**, never presented as a wallet.

### Gear
Fixed authored sidegrades and recipes over random affix farming. No gear score, armor ladder, or “better gear” phrasing. Two weapons + two addons; Boss Relic consumes one addon slot; one family signature; one optional Fusion.

## Difficulty, profiles, records
Do not ship selectable “Easy/Hard” profiles or all-time global boards now.

**Difficulty = Archive Contracts:** after a first clear, authored conditions add techniques/pattern pressure, not HP sponges. Same player foundation underneath.

**Profile card shows proof, not power:** regions reached, bosses cleared, mastery emblems, personal best contracts, deepest descent, runs. Lifetime kills/Coins may remain history but never rank players.

**Records rollout:**
1. Personal bests in Archive.
2. Normalized fixed-seed weekly contracts after authoritative scoring.
3. Separate solo/party boards; fixed starter loadout/pool; Heartwork disabled/normalized; ruleset versioned.
4. Score boss completion, time, downs, and optional mastery objective. Rewards are titles/Camp trophies/cosmetics only. No Amber, gear, or power.

Global/all-time leaderboards wait for server-authoritative anti-cheat, score formula, party normalization, and versioning.

## Explicit cuts / reshapes
### Cut now
- Gilded Warden as mandatory launch boss (demote to optional Amber Bailiff lieutenant).
- Combat pets.
- Player XP/levels/item levels/mastery XP.
- Extra currencies/resources/tokens/keys-as-currency.
- Ammo economy, armor, gear score, active-item fifth slot.
- Random vertical affix tiers and duplicate-to-Coins conversion.
- Generic “better gear” language.
- Full terrain destruction/building, survival meters, gathering/crafting economy.
- Tempered Shot II and future Foundation ranks.
- Generic circle/ring/ellipse/radial art; mechanical radii remain invisible/materialized.

### Defer
- Global/all-time leaderboards.
- Four Arena templates except Boss+Lieutenant.
- Gas, secret walls, advanced brazier fire.
- Shared-base defense, day/night, freeform building.
- Public/social Camp.
- Combat companions of any kind.

### Merge
- Rootkite into later Verdant elite unless necessary.
- Knellbat into Bat-family leadership ecology.
- Weapon Rack + Addon Bench + Foundation into Armorer.
- Training + Practice Range into Practice Yard.
- Resonance selection into Amber Heart.
- Fusion Forge into Jet-gated Archive expansion.

## Coherence gate for every future feature
1. Which existing universal rule does it build on?
2. Which region/material/ecology makes it belong?
3. Is it temporary expression, horizontal unlock, bounded home growth, or sidegrade?
4. What new moment-to-moment decision does it create?
5. Can silhouette/material communicate it without circles or generic particles?
6. What is its one genuine new hook, and what does it reuse?
7. Which existing incentive channel owns its reward?
8. If the external inspiration and proper nouns disappeared, would it still unmistakably be blobrogue?

Fail two questions: reshape. Fail three: cut.

# MAIN RUNNER DECISIONS — ACCEPTED SPINE (OVERRIDES RECOMMENDATIONS ABOVE)
**Status: locked by the main blobrogue project runner. Future creative critique must operate inside this spine unless implementation evidence requires reopening it. Do not silently revive superseded cuts.**

## Adopted
- Keep the **six-region material/hazard spine** and one universal hazard contract. Region names/order should follow current approved encounter integration; do not rename or remove live/approved content merely for conceptual neatness.
- Consolidate the persistent hub as **Amber Camp** with merged readable stations/functions.
- Keep the Dealer world hook: **Patch**, a salvage-hauler appearing at secured relay niches. No Dealer reputation/currency subsystem.
- Use **Heartwork** for bounded permanent Amber choices. No rank pile or unbounded vertical ladder.
- Enforce “no system pile”: one foundation underneath variety (shared movement verbs, hazards, statuses, Resonance, progression channels).

## Approved boss/curriculum chain — do not replace
`Slime King → F10 Gauntlet → Marrow → Weaver → Gilded Warden → Hollow Choir`

- The five named bosses remain the approved current content chain; F10 is a non-boss Arena Gauntlet milestone.
- **Jet remains later endgame content**, not a replacement for a completed/approved boss.
- Do not demote/remove Gilded Warden based on role-overlap theory alone. Critique it through implementation evidence: readability, TTK, fight overlap in actual play, art identity, or progression failure.
- **Ashen Choir** is a held rename candidate only. Do not rename Hollow Choir until content integration shows the Hollow-family vocabulary collision materially confuses players or production.
- Current approved content names and placements stay unless implementation evidence demands change.

## Pets remain — strict scope
Pets are **account-unlocked utility**, not cut.

Hard constraints:
- max one pet/player;
- no collision, body blocking, aggro target, loot pickup, or projectile spam;
- one low-frequency utility verb with clear owner-colored authored tell and ≥6s cooldown;
- target ≤10% sustained DPS, preferably zero-DPS utility;
- no pet currency, pet meter, pet gear slot, rarity ladder, level track, or vertical upgrade tree;
- in four-player combat, pet visuals must collapse/quiet under effect-density limits rather than obscure enemy telegraphs;
- unlocks remain horizontal account flags / boss or mastery rewards.

Future critique question: does the pet create one legible co-op decision without becoming a fifth player-body or another progression economy?

## Difficulty remains — three profiles
**Casual / Standard / Brutal** remain approved.

Creative/readability guardrails:
- Casual improves accessibility/readability and forgiveness without removing core attack grammar.
- Standard is canonical tuning.
- Brutal increases composition sophistication, technique combinations, commitment pressure, and recovery demands before raw HP/damage.
- No exclusive power unlocks by difficulty; recognition/cosmetics are acceptable.
- Bosses remain technique/pattern tests, not HP sponges.
- Accessibility settings remain distinct from profile prestige where appropriate.

Future critique question: does each profile change the decisions players make, or merely inflate/deflate stats?

## Global boards remain — authoritative and normalized
Global leaderboards are approved **after authoritative scoring** and are not cut.

Hard constraints:
- normalize/segment solo and party play;
- fixed or versioned rulesets/seeds/loadout pools where competition requires comparability;
- normalize or disable Heartwork/account power for ranked boards;
- score authored performance (completion, time, downs, mastery objective), not lifetime account age;
- version and retire boards after balance changes;
- rewards are recognition/cosmetics/trophies/titles, not Amber, gear, or combat power;
- anti-cheat and server-authoritative event capture are prerequisites.

Future critique question: does the board compare skill under equivalent rules, or merely measure account age, party size, or obsolete balance?

## Future critique protocol
1. First check whether the proposal conflicts with this Accepted Spine.
2. If yes, do not reopen the decision on taste alone.
3. Bring implementation evidence: unreadable combat, duplicated player decision, technical infeasibility, measured engagement/retention issue, progression dead-end, or failed playtest.
4. Prefer reshape within the approved system before cut/rename/removal.
5. Main runner owns any exception or reopening.
