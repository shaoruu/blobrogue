# blobrogue — DARK FAMILY RE-THESIS (Creative Director recommendation)

## Strongest framework call: RESONANCE
**Resonance** is the universal charged signature-technique system. It belongs to blobrogue because the hero is living amber: every weapon, blessing, and kill-chain makes the blob’s body vibrate with the family currently in hand. The combo meter charges Resonance; activating it lets the equipped family briefly make the whole room obey its signature rule. Same meter, same input, same progression source, same duration contract for every family. Only payload + authored presentation vary.

Why this wins over Overdrive/Manifestation:
- **Overdrive** sounds technological and biases every family toward machines.
- **Manifestation** is fantasy-generic and lore-heavy.
- **Resonance** connects directly to amber as a material, audio/music, co-op synchronization, combo rhythm, and the idea that the blob physically carries weapon identities. It is original to our world and mechanically descriptive.

Suggested HUD language: `RESONANCE READY` / `RESONATING: HOLLOW` / meter icon = a small cracked amber tuning-shard (authored silhouette, not a ring).

## Dark family: THE HOLLOW
**Hollow amber** is amber that has had its warmth and memory extracted, leaving black resin shot through with dying orange veins. The family’s weapons are salvaged from failed blobs and ruined expeditions; they bank hurt, return it violently, and reward fighting near death. This grows directly from the soft warm amber hero vs. cold corrupted world identity.

Universal mechanics preserved:
- status = **FRACTURE**: store damage, detonate on re-hit/death;
- risk = self-bleed that never takes the last HP;
- low-HP bonus = +20% Hollow damage under 33% HP;
- Resonance payload = auto-bank hurt + guaranteed detonation for ~4s.

### Original weapon names
1. **WIDOWBITE** — black-resin longsword with a chipped amber nerve. Swing leaves a lingering tear in space.
2. **BLEAKSEED** — palm-sized dead-amber pod that sheds homing splinters; costs HP every third burst, refunds on kill.
3. **RUINBREATH** — cracked censer/blower that exhales razor-thin black resin flakes in a cone, rapidly banking hurt.
4. **BLACK LANTERN** — a caged dead-amber light; charge it, release a heavy resin heart that cracks into seeking splinters.

## Signature Resonance: THE LIGHT GOES OUT
For four seconds, warmth is pulled out of the room. Colors desaturate toward soot and cold blue-black; the hero’s surviving amber seams become the only warm color. Every enemy develops visible black-resin stress cracks; hits snap those seams and detonate banked hurt.

No shrine, Japanese ritual language, “domain,” magic ring, or imported iconography. The room is not transported elsewhere — the **existing world is robbed of warmth**, which directly dramatizes our core identity.

## Revised original art thesis: THE WORLD DRAINS COLD
- The effect begins from the hero’s amber body: warm light gutters inward like a lamp starving for fuel.
- Near-black resin **veins and fracture planes** propagate through floors/walls following existing architecture and tile seams. They are material cracks, not random VFX.
- A few large, authored **dead-amber slabs/splinters** intrude from edges with clear material thickness; no rings, bubbles, runes, or shrine motifs.
- Hero readability comes from a jagged surviving island of warm amber light shaped by the actual floor/terrain, not a clean pocket mask.
- Bright violet is removed as the dominant identity. Palette = soot-black, cold desaturated blue, dead-resin brown, with scarce dying amber (#c77320). Optional faint violet only in stress-crack cores if needed for damage readability.
- FRACTURE overlay becomes black resin seams crawling across the enemy sprite, with 2–3 attached amber splinters under tension. Detonation is those seams snapping and ejecting unequal resin shards along the stored stress direction — no halo, knot, paper seal, or radial burst.
- Audio: room ambience low-passes, weapon sounds dry out, one amber “glass-hum” falls in pitch; detonations answer with brittle resin cracks. Original and tied to material.

The visual thesis in one sentence: **the hero weaponizes the moment warmth leaves amber.**
