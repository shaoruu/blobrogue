# blobrogue — ENCOUNTER / PROGRESSION CURRICULUM (canonical studio design gate)
**Design only; no gameplay implementation until authoritative multiplayer A→B→C is production-green and runner routes it.** Six authored regions THROUGH NULL, five mandatory bosses, exact floor curriculum, encounter deck, family grammar, rare/miniboss cadence, Archive Contracts, co-op recovery, and Camp-Companion scope. Mechanics preserve the approved teaching→remix→prove structure.

## 0. World spine / boss cadence (locked)
Thirty floors = six 5-floor regions:
1. F1–5 **AMBERWILD** → The Slime King.
2. F6–10 **SUNLESS CAVES** → Marrow.
3. F11–15 **THE DEEP** → The Weaver.
4. F16–20 **EMBERREACH** → The Ashen Choir.
5. F21–25 **GILDED SCAR** → Arena milestone / optional Amber Bailiff lieutenant, NO mandatory boss.
6. F26–30 **NULL** → Jet final boss.
Mandatory boss chain: `Slime King → Marrow → Weaver → Ashen Choir → Jet`. F25 intentionally breaks cadence; five bosses gate six places. Jet resolves the core amber/jet story and unlocks Fusions/mastery post-clear.

Rootbound Warrens is NOT a biome. Its dense-root rooms/formations become an Amberwild F3–4 room family and later Wilds nodes. Gilded Archive is replaced by Gilded Scar: failed amber civilization, rigid/fossilized—not clean orderly fantasy.

## 1. Universal small-mob family grammar
Reusable verbs, not separate systems per sprite:
- **HUNT:** closes and commits (Charger is authored HUNT).
- **ORBIT:** holds flank/radius, looks for opening (Bat/Seamwalker).
- **BURROW:** leaves plane with continuous material track, locks return (Rattleback).
- **ANCHOR:** claims/redirects space (Bellows, fossil turret).
- **FLOCK:** group separation/rally/surge (Slime, Rootkite elite).
- **FLEE/BAIT:** retreats toward support then turns (Knellbat).
- **SHIELD** is a combat posture on HUNT/ANCHOR, not a seventh movement engine.
One primary verb + max one escalation modifier. Large/Brute/Elite are tiers layered on a family.

## 2. Thirty-floor learning curve (one new lesson max/floor)
### F1–5 AMBERWILD — living/elastic boundary
- **F1 Establish:** simple HUNT Slimes; round/open root rooms, crates/pots; objective/exit clarity. No tier/hazard.
- **F2 Discover:** FLOCK Slime ring→surge + guaranteed melee. Room loops preserve walking escape.
- **F3 Choose:** Bat ORBIT→dive introduced with simple Chasers; first Patch Dealer relay. **Snaproot** introduced in one authored room: coil/leaf direction → spring impulse → slack/destructible recovery. No generic root-slow field.
- **F4 Synthesize / Rootbound room family:** Slime flock + Bat orbit; denser branching roots, Snaproot repositioning. Rootkite may appear only as rare elite if silhouette variety is needed—not a common new family.
- **F5 Prove — Slime King:** Summoner/AoE baseline; adds use learned FLOCK. No other complex family.
Lesson: telegraph, pack priority, melee discovery, Dealer choice, punish recovery.

### F6–10 SUNLESS CAVES — sound / momentum
- **F6 Arrive:** Orbit-Dive Bat in shale/echo chambers; lower pressure after boss; Patch relay. No new family.
- **F7 Decide Pursuit:** Knellbat FLEE/BAIT leader with simple Bat pack; kill leader→1.2s formation break. **Echo Shale** introduced: player movement/shot emits directional material fracture/noise event; one strong impact breaks it.
- **F8 Read Ground:** Rattleback BURROW introduced alone with simple Chasers; lifted shale chevron→rupture teeth→erupt.
- **F9 Commit:** non-boss Charger introduced alone in long wall-impact lanes; Patch preparation. No Knellbat/Rattleback same first room.
- **F10 Prove — Marrow:** blind SOUND HUNT/charge. Authoritative noise/velocity, never raw input; stand still/mislead/bait wall. No Charger adds.
Lesson: last-known position, intentional pursuit, wall consequence, sound/material tells.

### F11–15 THE DEEP — fracture / wrong geometry
- **F11 Arrive:** Crookleg wall-pounce introduced alone with simple HUNT; offset/T rooms and valid wall anchors.
- **F12 Track:** Seamwalker ORBIT/path-controller introduced alone; 3–4 visible resin/mineral path segments, junction pause.
- **F13 Counter:** Deep front-Shielder introduced with one simple Orbiter; side/melee/pierce routes; Patch relay. **Load Seam** banks impacts then directionally snaps debris/lane (world material, not combat Fracture status).
- **F14 Synthesize:** Crookleg + Seamwalker, max one each; lattice/destructible seam gives secondary punish. No Rattleback.
- **F15 Prove — The Weaver:** mobile duel/pattern reading. Displacement follows visible seams; fragile simple adds only if needed P2.
Lesson: future geometry, wall orientation, catch recoveries, destroy pattern infrastructure.

### F16–20 EMBERREACH — false warmth / pressure cycles
- **F16 Arrive:** Cinderjack jet-HUNT introduced; rear vents glow→locked burst→cool exposed core. Pressure Vent material introduced alone.
- **F17 Anchor:** Bellows ANCHOR introduced alone; directional thermal lane→deflate/reposition. Destructible vent caps can redirect next burst.
- **F18 Decide Route:** Cinderjack + one Pressure Vent OR Bellows + simple Chasers; Patch relay. Never both complex mobs first composition.
- **F19 Synthesize:** Cinderjack + Bellows under commit scheduler; one pushes route, one commits; no simultaneous full denial. Voice-fragment silhouettes preview boss song, not combat adds yet.
- **F20 Prove — The Ashen Choir:** grieving artillery/pressure raid. Fulgurite cracks, sung wedges, vitrifying seams; few song-linked voice fragments, not swarm spam.
Lesson: read heat pressure/cooling cycles, move between authored safe pockets, coordinate raid space.

### F21–25 GILDED SCAR — failed amber threshold / no mandatory boss
- **F21 Arrive:** fossil front-Shielder introduced; dead honey/brass/fossil shelves, clear side routes. Lower pressure after Choir.
- **F22 Anchor:** fossil ANCHOR construct/turret introduced alone; marked material firing lane. **Dead Prism** introduced: rotate once or shatter; redirects one pressure/projectile family then becomes cover/debris.
- **F23 Break Killbox:** Shielder + Anchor; movable/destructible cover, two flank routes; Patch relay.
- **F24 Synthesize / choice:** normal Shielder/Orbiter deck OR optional side-arena telegraph for **Amber Bailiff** lieutenant. Bailiff is optional Goldwork/mastery gate, not mandatory progression.
- **F25 Arena Milestone — Final Seal:** sequential authored gauntlet (Shielder commander → Anchor lieutenant → mixed elite) with 1.2s breath beats, OR Boss+Lieutenant template if Bailiff path was chosen. Clearing final seal opens Null regardless of optional Bailiff. Premium reward.
Lesson: dismantle rigid systems, use cover/angles, endure without a mandatory sixth boss.

### F26–30 NULL — subtraction / final synthesis, NO new family/status/meter
Bodies/materials become sparse, but tells remain fully readable. Null remixes learned verbs by removing affordances, not adding a Null system.
- **F26 Subtract Cover:** simple HUNT/FLOCK in sparse stable lanes; **Blank Shear** introduced: material seam points→commits→removes one cover/hazard affordance→leaves stable empty lane.
- **F27 Subtract Route:** ORBIT/Seamwalker remix with fewer path junctions, stronger preview; no new move.
- **F28 Subtract Safety:** one Rattleback OR one Crookleg with simple HUNT; Blank Shear removes a known safe object only after readable directional tell.
- **F29 Reflect:** curated two-family mastery composition drawn from player-known verbs; last recovery/prep relay is an abandoned secured Patch cache (no new NPC/system). No Arena.
- **F30 Prove — Jet:** curated, counter-readable family rotation; no copying entire build. Resonance drains existing warmth/material; every technique uses learned telegraph grammar. Final clear unlocks Fusions + Archive mastery/adaptive contracts + visible Amber Heart restoration.
Lesson: mastery through recognition under subtraction, not surprise mechanics.

## 3. Composition synergy / overlap arbitration
Allowed complementary pairs:
- FLOCK + ORBIT (center vs flank).
- FLEE/BAIT + one HUNT Charger (pursuit choice vs lane), scheduler-controlled.
- SHIELD + ANCHOR (angle/priority) only with two flank routes.
- BURROW + simple HUNT.
- wall-pounce + ORBIT, max one each.
- ANCHOR + jet-HUNT only with authored escape pocket.

Banned:
- Burrow + wall-pounce small room; duplicate Chargers/Burrowers/Anchors in introduction band; Shielder corridor without flank; >2 complex families/room; matching boss/add verb; simultaneous locks leaving <0.4s or no walking escape.
Arena/global scheduler: max2 major committed patterns +1 arena-wide denial; delay/reject conflicting reservations.

## 4. Encounter deck / anti-repetition
Each region: 8–10 authored cards (families + room archetype + hazard/material + reward weight), deterministic shuffle-bag:
- Exact card cooldown4 combat rooms.
- Same primary complex family max2 consecutive.
- Same room archetype max2 consecutive.
- Material interaction cooldown2 rooms.
- Breather simple room after boss/Arena/elite-double.
- ≥30% simple/mastery rooms; ≤35% two-complex rooms.
- If generator cannot satisfy, drop complexity/material interaction before repeating high-pressure card.
Patch/Dealer, boss, and Arena do not stack on one floor except authored F25 branch.

## 5. Tiers / rare / miniboss
Canonical threat costs/TTKs from Balance Reset:
- Large: readability/body variant, no new mechanic.
- Brute: one authored heavy technique; costs2.5, TTK1.8–3.2s.
- Elite: one visible modifier; costs4, TTK3–6s, never blanket damage.
- Rare: 8–12% eligible replacement within threat budget, one region-native modifier, premium Coins/upgrade chance only.
- Miniboss: authored Arena/gauntlet only, 45–70% boss effective HP, 1–2 moves; never random normal-room roll.

## 7. Difficulty expression — Archive Contracts, not launch mode menu
Cut selectable global Veteran from canonical launch scope. First clear unlocks **Archive Contracts** with normalized authored rules:
- Composition/pattern conditions, fixed seed/loadout where competitive, canonical recovery minimums; no hidden damage/HP ladder.
- Same progression unlocks. Rewards = mastery proof, titles, Camp trophies/cosmetics; no bonus Amber/gear/power.
- Accessibility assists remain separate and never block unlocks.
Internal Veteran profile may remain a QA/load-test preset only, not player-facing launch mode.

## 8. Co-op recovery / spectate / deterministic record
Post-authoritative Stage C:
- Downed; revive1.5s uninterrupted, damage cancels; restore2HP, protect1.0s, no attack first.35s.
- Bleedout20s→spectate; all down/spectating ends run. Floor clear/boss death returns spectators at exit/reward with2HP.
- Spectate living teammate only/interest-bounded; ping CD3s; no pickup/aggro/scouting.
- Premium boss/Arena choices instanced; world weapons shared; boss/region unlock flags for every eligible participating party member, no DPS loot competition.
- Record deterministic server data now; replay viewer/rematch is post-C polish and not a gameplay gate.

## 9. Camp Companions only — combat pets cut
Canonical launch scope permits **Camp Companions only**: cosmetic inhabitants earned by boss/mastery flags. No pet slot, rarity, levels, stats, currency, AI, combat, following player, or roadmap promise of combat pets. Any future combat companion requires a new studio gate after readability/network budgets; it is not quasi-approved here.

## 10. Material / art rules
- No generic circle/ring/ellipse/radial telegraphs. Internal radii remain mechanical; art uses material evidence.
- F4 root interaction is **Snaproot**: coil/leaf direction, spring commit, slack destructible recovery—not “root-snag slow.”
- Marrow/Chargers: bone/shale hearing flare + directional debris.
- Ashen Choir: fulgurite cracks, sung uneven wedges, vitrified heat seams; rename Hollow Choir everywhere (Hollow reserved for family).
- Amber Bailiff/Gilded Scar: fossil shelves, resin seams, embedded relic silhouettes—not glyph/rune/prison rings.
- Null: sparse authored subtraction while silhouettes/commit tells remain readable; never low-contrast emptiness.

## 11. Overload / incoherence fixes
1. Six regions/five bosses: F25 Arena threshold solves cadence; Jet mandatory finale, not postgame.
2. Rootbound is Amberwild room family, not duplicate green biome.
3. Warden demoted/renamed optional Amber Bailiff; Goldwork unlock optional, Null gate independent.
4. Hollow reserved for family; boss renamed Ashen Choir.
5. Selectable Veteran cut; Archive Contracts own post-clear challenge/records.
6. Combat pets cut entirely; Camp Companions cosmetic only.
7. Six hazard engines rejected: all use `material source→directional tell→commit→pressure→recovery→optional destruction` (Snaproot/Echo Shale/Load Seam/Pressure Vent/Dead Prism/Blank Shear).
8. Circle/ring art rejected; materialized tells replace it.
9. HP/tier/region multipliers cannot override focused TTK gates.

## Density check / exact conflict
The remap is feasible under max-one-new-lesson/floor, but **Sunless is densest**: F7 Knellbat, F8 Rattleback, F9 Charger, F10 Marrow. That is three new families in three floors before their boss. Fix is explicit: Bat ORBIT was learned in Amberwild F3 and carries in at F6; Knellbat is an elite Bat-family leadership modifier (not a new universal verb); Rattleback BURROW is the only genuinely new movement module; Charger HUNT uses existing lunge/commit tech. If playtests still overload F7–9, move Rattleback first appearance to optional F8 side-room and guarantee mastery on F11 Deep arrival—do NOT cut boss cadence or add another tutorial floor.

## Bottom line
Keep the approved encounter mechanics, but the world tells one corruption story: Amberwild life → Sunless sound → Deep fracture → Ember false warmth → Gilded failed amber → Null subtraction. Five bosses gate six regions; F25 Arena is the deliberate threshold. Jet is the finale. Challenge becomes Archive Contracts; pets are Camp-only; no ring art or duplicate progression systems.
