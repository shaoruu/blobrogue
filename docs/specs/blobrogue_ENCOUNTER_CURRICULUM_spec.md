## 1. Universal small-mob families
Reusable verbs, one primary + max one escalation:
- **HUNT / Charger** — closes, locks, commits; wall/terrain consequence.
- **ORBIT / Orbiter** — holds flank/radius, looks for opening.
- **BURROW / Burrower** — leaves plane, continuous material track, locks destination.
- **ANCHOR / Zoner** — claims space and forces route choice.
- **FLOCK** — group separation/rally/surge.
- **FLEE/BAIT** — retreats toward support, then commits.
- **SHIELD** — frontal combat posture layered on HUNT/ANCHOR, not a movement engine.
Large/Brute/Elite are tiers, not new AI families.

## 2. Floor-by-floor curriculum (one new lesson max/floor)

- **F5 Prove — Slime King:** summoner/AoE baseline; adds use learned FLOCK, no other complex family.

## 3. Composition synergy / overlap arbitration
Allowed: FLOCK+ORBIT; FLEE/BAIT+one Charger; SHIELD+ANCHOR only with two flank routes; BURROW+simple HUNT; wall-pounce+ORBIT max1 each; ANCHOR+jet-HUNT only with escape pocket.
Banned: Burrow+wall-pounce small room; duplicate complex family in intro; Shielder corridor without flank; >2 complex families; matching boss/add verb; simultaneous locks leaving <0.4s/no walking escape.
Global scheduler: max2 major committed patterns +1 arena-wide denial; delay/reject conflicting reservations.

## 4. Encounter deck / anti-repetition
Per region 8–10 authored cards (family composition + room archetype + material/hazard + reward), deterministic shuffle bag:
- Exact card cooldown4 rooms; primary complex max2 consecutive; room archetype max2; material interaction cooldown2.
- Breather simple room after Arena/boss/elite-double.
- ≥30% simple/mastery rooms; ≤35% two-complex rooms.
- If constraints fail, drop complexity/hazard before repeating pressure card.
Patch, boss, and Arena never stack on same floor except authored F29 preparation flow.

## 5. Tiers / rares / minibosses
Balance Reset is numerical authority.
- Large: body/readability only, no mechanic.
- Brute: one authored heavy technique, TTK1.8–3.2s.
